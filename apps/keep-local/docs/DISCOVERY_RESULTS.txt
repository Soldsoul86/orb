Limitations and unverified work
------------------------------
No physical device was attached. Phone installation, actual user-recording
preservation, Play Protect behavior, real-library usefulness, battery/thermals,
large-library latency and recipient-specific uploads remain UNVERIFIED.
Single-item sharing/export is instrumented; Review's multi-item sharing is
implemented but third-party interoperability is not verified.
All review decisions above are automated fixture decisions. They do not establish
useful discoveries accepted per 100 real assets, dismissal preference, time saved,
or a general precision/recall rate. No action recognition or identity inference.

Saved-audio transcription is INCOMPLETE/unsupported; audio metadata/playback/export
work. English literal document rules and Latin OCR are limited. PDF OCR covers
only the first 20 pages; UTF-8 text/DOCX limits and unsupported DOC/XLS/PPT/archive,
encrypted/non-seekable formats are detailed in DISCOVERY_04.txt. New authorized
photos/videos use CLIP and metadata; automatic OCR for those external visual
sources is not added. Existing private-import OCR remains available.

Similarity thresholds are uncalibrated and candidate batches are bounded, not
exhaustive all-pairs retrieval. Experience groups can miss page-boundary relations.
Folder exclusions use explicit path/document-ID prefixes. URI aliases can represent
the same physical file; references are idempotent per URI and visual-pair suppression
uses exact byte hashes. Sources changed without provider size/modification changes
need a derived-index rebuild. Storage indicators show database and model components;
thumbnail-cache usage is not separately presented. CPU inference is verified;
GPU/NPU acceleration and real-device energy gains are not claimed.

The unrelated Orb 'verify' workflow still fails in scripts/simulate-hard-exit.mjs
at event.risk.measured.toFixed. Keep Local's dedicated build/sign/emulator pipelines
pass. No unrelated trading-runtime source was changed.

Next smallest improvement: an opt-in physical-phone pilot with a small personal
