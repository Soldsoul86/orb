Keep Local 0.4 — remaining physical-device acceptance

No physical Android device was attached during development. Emulator results
cannot establish battery life, thermals, Play Protect status or compatibility
with a particular phone or third-party receiving app.

1. Keep the existing application installed. Install the supplied 0.4.0 APK over
   com.keep.local.next / 0.3.0. If Android rejects the update, retain the app and
   report the exact installer message; do not uninstall to resolve a mismatch.
   The separate older com.keep.local application is not accessed or upgraded.
2. Open several existing recordings and confirm playback, titles and notes.
   Confirm the previously installed visual model pack is still available.
3. In Settings, authorize only desired categories and a small document folder.
   Test denial of another category and Android's selected-photo access option.
4. Start indexing, pause, resume, close and reopen the application. Search while
   indexing is incomplete. Observe progress, storage and responsiveness.
5. In airplane mode, open Worth Reviewing. Inspect the source evidence, Keep
   one useful suggestion, Correct another and Dismiss an unwanted/duplicate one.
   Restart and confirm the decisions persist. Check that dismissal stays suppressed.
6. Play a timestamped video source. Test single and multiple-item Sharesheet
   attachments in the actual receiving apps. Save a video to Gallery/Files and
   attach it manually when a receiver does not accept the shared content URI.
   A Sharesheet launch alone does not prove a successful third-party upload.
7. Revoke the document folder through Manage authorized sources and revoke a
   category in Android permission settings. Confirm unavailable sources and their
   derived content disappear from search/review. Originals must remain unchanged.
8. Remove the derived index. Confirm originals, notes, models and human decisions
   remain; rebuild only if desired. Do not use Android Clear storage for this test.
9. With a larger consented library, measure time to first useful discovery,
   accepted/acted-on discoveries per 100 indexed assets, dismissal rate, unsupported
   claims, indexing time, peak memory, storage growth, battery drain and thermals.

Do not disable Play Protect or device security protections. English literal
rules, Latin PDF OCR, sampled-frame CLIP similarity and bounded candidate batches
have the limits described in DISCOVERY_04.txt. No action/identity inference,
saved-audio transcription or new model is claimed.

Recommended next smallest improvement: run a small opt-in personal-library pilot,
label accepted/dismissed suggestions, and tune queue priorities and similarity
thresholds from that evidence before adding another model or discovery category.
